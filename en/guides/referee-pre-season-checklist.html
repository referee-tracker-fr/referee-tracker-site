Getting ready for your refereeing season

Paperwork, kit, fitness, dates to block out: the list to start the season without stress.

1Paperwork to update

  • Your licenceRenew it as soon as registration opens: no licence, no appointments.
  • The medical fileCertificate or health questionnaire, depending on your region's rules.
  • Your bank detailsUp to date with every organiser that pays you.
  • Your contact detailsAddress, phone, email: a lost appointment means a missed game.
  • Your carInsurance, roadworthiness test and registration up to date.

2Kit

  • UniformShirts, trousers and shoes in good condition, and this season's required uniform.
  • Two whistles and a watchIn good condition and reliable.
  • A bag always packedKit, spare clothes, towel, water, something to write with.

Order what is missing before the restart: in September, delivery times get longer.

3Getting fit again

A referee runs several kilometres per game. Start again a few weeks before the season: jogging, short intervals, changes of direction. If fitness tests are planned, practise their format in advance.

4The start-of-season calendar

  • Clinics and meetingsOften compulsory: write them down as soon as they are announced.
  • Written or fitness testsBlock the date and revise the rules that changed.
  • Your unavailabilityHolidays, exams, work: declare it early.
  • Your goalsNumber of games, target level, things to work on.

5Your season budget

Uniform, licence, fuel, tolls, sometimes a hotel: refereeing costs before it pays. At the start of the season, estimate your target number of games, the average distance and the fuel price.

If you referee a lot, watch the total of your fees: it is counted from 1 January to 31 December.

Getting ready for your refereeing season

Before your first game comes the restart: paperwork, kit, fitness, dates to block out. Here is the list to start the season without stress.

Paperwork to update

  • Your licence: renew it as soon as registration opens. No validated licence, no appointments.
  • The medical file your region or county asks for: certificate or health questionnaire, depending on the rules in force.
  • Your bank details: check they are up to date with every organiser that pays you, or your fees get lost.
  • Your contact details: address, phone, email. An appointment sent to an old address means a missed game.
  • Your car: insurance, roadworthiness test and registration up to date if you drive to games.

Kit

  • Uniform: check the state of your shirts, trousers and shoes, and the uniform required for the new season.
  • Two whistles in good condition and a reliable watch.
  • A packed bag: full kit, spare clothes, towel, water, something to write with.

Order what is missing before the restart: in September, delivery times get longer.

Getting fit again

A referee runs several kilometres per game. Start again a few weeks before the season: jogging, short intervals, changes of direction. If fitness tests are planned at the start of the season, practise their format in advance.

The start-of-season calendar

  • Clinics and start-of-season meetings: write them down as soon as they are announced, they are often compulsory.
  • Written or fitness tests: block the date and revise the rules that changed.
  • Your unavailability: holidays, exams, work. Declare it early to avoid impossible appointments.
  • Your goals: number of games, target level, things to work on. Write them down: you will read them again at the end of the season.

Your season budget

Refereeing costs money before it brings any in: uniform, shoes, licence, fuel, tolls, sometimes a hotel night for a game far from home. Make a simple estimate at the start of the season: the number of games you aim for, the average trip distance and the fuel price. You will know what to expect and spot a late payment, or a trip that costs more than it pays, much faster.

Keep the calendar year in mind too: if you referee a lot, watch the total of your fees, which is counted from 1 January to 31 December.

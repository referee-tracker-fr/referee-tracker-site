Why these guides?

Refereeing a game also means everything before and after it: receiving your appointments, planning your travel, checking your match fees, tracking your payments and improving. These guides are written by an active referee, from county level to national championships, in 5x5 and 3x3.

Each guide explains the method by hand, then what a referee tracking app can do for you. Amounts quoted are examples: your appointment and your committee's or league's fee scale remain the reference.

1Why review your games on video

  • Check your calls with a cool headWhat you called, and what you should have called
  • Work on your positioningAnd your rotations with your partner
  • Prepare a talk with your referee coach or assessorWith clips to back it up
  • Keep a record of your progressFrom one game to the next

2How REF CLIPS works

  • You open your game videoFrom your computer or from a link sent by the club (Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer)
  • You type the call code on the keyboardWhile it plays: "travelling", "unsportsmanlike"… Press Enter and the moment is tagged with the right quarter
  • You draw on the pictureAn arrow, a circle round your position, and the video holds on your drawing for a few seconds
  • REF CLIPS cuts each play into a clipUp to 4K, and creates a clear PDF report of your game

5Filming your game for analysis

  • Camera up highLevel with the half-court line, to see the whole court and every official
  • A wide, steady shotA tripod or a seat in the stands avoids shaky footage
  • The whole gameIncluding time-outs: what happens between officials matters too
  • The original fileRather than a compressed copy: positioning details stay visible

With good footage, analysis goes faster: every play you tag in REF CLIPS becomes a sharp clip your referee coach can comment on precisely.

6Frequently asked questions

How do I review a refereed game on video?

Watch your game and tag each call as it happens, then go back over the plays one by one. REF CLIPS automates this: a keyboard code tags the play, the app cuts the clip and prepares a PDF report.

What video analysis software is there for basketball referees?

REF CLIPS is a video analysis app built for basketball referees, on Mac and Windows: keyboard tagging, precise clips, drawing on the picture and a PDF report.

Can I use the video a club sends me?

Yes: paste the Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer link and REF CLIPS fetches the video.

Do I need a separate account for REF CLIPS?

No. REF CLIPS opens with your Referee Tracker account, and your games show up straight away.

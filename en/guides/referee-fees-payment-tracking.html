Tracking your basketball referee match fees and payments

County committee, regional league, federation, clubs: a basketball referee gets paid by several bodies, each on its own schedule.

1The problem: several payers, several schedules

  • Different payersDepending on the level of the game: county committee, regional league, federation, or the home club directly
  • Different due datesPaid on the night of the game, at the end of the month, every quarter…
  • Grouped transfersWith no breakdown: one lump sum for several games, and it is up to you to guess which
  • RoundingOn mileage that shifts the amount by a few cents

2The method in 4 steps

  • For each game, note the expected match feeThe fixed fee plus round-trip mileage using your rate scale. The full calculation is in our guide to basketball referee match fees
  • Link a payer and an expected payment dateBased on each body's payment schedule
  • Each time a transfer comes in, tick off the games paid. For a grouped transfer, look for the combination of games whose total matches to the cent
  • Keep an eye on late paymentsA payment past its expected date should be chased quickly, before the trail goes cold

6Frequently asked questions

How do I know which games a grouped transfer covers?

Add up the match fees of the unpaid games from the same payer until you hit the exact amount. Referee Tracker does it for you: you enter who paid you and how much, and the app finds the combination of games.

Who pays a basketball referee?

It depends on the level and the competition: the county committee, the regional league, the federation or the home club. Each one pays on its own schedule.

What should I do if a referee payment is late?

First check the payer's expected payment date, then chase them with the date and the game number. Referee Tracker alerts you as soon as a payment goes past its expected date.

Does the mileage allowance cover fuel?

Not always exactly: it is a flat rate, while the real cost depends on your car and the price of fuel. The app works out both to give you your real income.

Referee match fees and your tax return

In most cases you have nothing to declare, thanks to an exemption threshold. Here is how to know where you stand.

In short

  • A threshold14.5% of the social security ceiling
  • Over the calendar year1 January to 31 December
  • RecordsKeep them for several years

1The rule: an exemption threshold

In France, money received by a referee who is not employed by their federation benefits from an allowance. Below the threshold, over a calendar year and all organisers together, it is exempt from:

  • Social contributions, CSG and CRDSArticle L. 241-16 of the Social Security Code
  • Income taxArticle 93 of the General Tax Code

2The figures

Money received in 2025€6,829.50declared in 2026 · ceiling €47,100
Money received in 2026€6,968.70declared in 2027 · ceiling €48,060

The social security ceiling changes every year: check it on the Urssaf website.

3Calendar year, not season

Your season runs from September to June, but the threshold is counted from 1 January to 31 December. Add up everything you received during the year, whatever season the games belong to.

A fee often arrives several weeks after the game. The date you receive the money is what counts.

4What if you go over?

It is no longer fully exempt. For the Urssaf, only the part above the threshold is normally subject to contributions. For income tax, specific rules apply: check impots.gouv.fr or ask your tax office.

5Records to keep

  • Payment statementsFrom each organiser
  • Bank statementsShowing the transfers
  • AppointmentsDate and venue of each game
  • Yearly summaryDate, organiser, amount

Please note: general explanations for France, not tax advice. For your situation, check impots.gouv.fr, the Urssaf or your tax office.
